# Mall Walk Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Walk from the street into Taipei 101's mall through the south entrance, walk its floors B1 to 5F among their real tenants, move between floors with an on-screen floor panel, and hand over to the damper chapter from it.

**Architecture:** The mall is a second **place** of the walk (groundwork merged in 62e682e): a lazily loaded `MallScene` with its own `MallSimulation`, registered through the same `PlaceApi` as the street. Moving between places is a new walk mode, **crossing**: a short fade to black during which the next place loads if needed and receives the visitor; the same crossing carries the floor panel's Damper button back to the tour. Tenants are baked from the official 101 feed into a dated JSON snapshot; floor geometry is built in code, in world coordinates inside the real podium outline.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 19, @react-three/fiber 9, drei, three 0.186, zustand, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-mall-walk-design.md`; terms in `CONTEXT.md` (Walk section).

This is plan 2 of 2; plan 1 (`2026-10-28-walk-groundwork.md`) is merged.

## Global Constraints

- Floors B1, 1F, 2F, 3F, 4F, 5F only. No 6F, no office floors, no walkable damper floor.
- Brand names on signs, never logos or monograms. Names stay in Latin script.
- Escalators are scenery: drawn (may animate), not walkable. The floor panel is the only way between floors.
- Performance: under 200 draw calls and 300k triangles in view (as `e2e/budget.spec.ts` checks). The mall is not in the initial download and loads the first time the visitor walks in.
- Assets are all code: procedural geometry, instancing, canvas-drawn text. No model files, no image files of brands.
- Flat low-poly style, matching the street.
- Reduced motion: the walk is not offered (unchanged), so neither is the mall.
- UI copy goes in `src/content/en.ts` like every other string (the app has no zh-Hant file yet; the owner reviews Chinese when it lands).
- Tests: behaviour in module tests on real data (`src/mall/tenants.json`); e2e only for wiring a browser alone shows. CI e2e shards stay well under 20 minutes.
- Domain words: visitor, crowd, walk, place, floor, floor panel, crossing. Not "player", "level", "room".
- No em dashes in code, comments, docs or commit messages. No `Co-Authored-By` or other AI attribution in commits.

## Decisions this plan makes (the spec left them to the builder)

- **Coordinates:** the mall uses world x/z like the street (east +x, north -z, tower centroid at the origin), inside `PODIUM.outline` (`src/scene/tower/podium.ts`). The tower base (square of half 27.3 m turned by `GRID_ROTATION`) stands inside the mall as a solid core. The podium is an L round the tower's west and north sides plus a strip south of it; the south entrance vestibule is x -6.5..5.5, z 30..42.8.
- **Floor heights** (metres above the 1F floor, which sits at the ground height at the entrance, `ground(0, 44)`): B1 -5.5, 1F 0, 2F 6.5, 3F 12, 4F 17.5, 5F 23. Ceilings 5 m above each floor (1F: 6 m).
- **Atrium:** a void through 2F, 3F, 4F (balustrade round it, blocks the visitor); 1F is the atrium floor (walkable, beauty counter islands on it); 5F is closed over it with the skylight above 5F; B1 has none.
- **Which floors draw:** the current floor only on B1 and 5F; all of 1F to 4F when the visitor is on any of them (they see each other across the atrium). No separate low-detail models: every floor is a few merged meshes, and the budget test proves the four together fit.
- **Crossing:** fade out 350 ms, wait (black, with a loading line) until the next place is registered, hand the visitor over, fade in 350 ms.
- **Street door:** walking into the south entrance means standing within `ENTRANCE = { x0: -6, x1: 5, z0: 42.8, z1: 43.6 }` (the visitor stops 0.3 m off the wall at z 42.8). Leaving the mall puts the visitor at `OUTSIDE_ENTRANCE = { x: -0.5, z: 45.5, yaw: Math.PI, pitch: 0 }` (facing south, out), outside that rect.
- **Damper handover:** the crossing ends in tour mode at the damper chapter's stop centre (`stopProgress(2)`), then the page scrolls there.

## Owner's acceptance checks (2026-10-29)

The owner asked for three things to be verified, not assumed. Each has tests below; none may be weakened to pass.

1. **The mall walk is the street walk.** Same keys, thumbstick, drag to look, Shift to hurry, same speeds (`WALK_SPEED` 4 m/s, `HURRY_SPEED` 8 m/s, `LOOK_RAD_PER_PX`), same HUD and exits. Unit `the walk on a mall floor is the street's walk` (Task 3); e2e `walking in the mall is the street's walk` (Task 5).
2. **No weird movement inside the building.** Never through a wall, shopfront, counter or balustrade, never into the atrium void, never onto another floor or off the floor's height; only the floor panel changes floors. Unit `no input ever puts the visitor through a wall, into the void, or onto another floor` and `every wall edge stops a visitor walking straight at it` (Task 3); e2e `pressing into walls and the balustrade never leaves the walk area` (Task 5).
3. **Every floor looks different.** Unit `no two floors share a look` (Task 2); e2e `each floor looks different` compares real rendered frames (Task 5), and the builder looks at every floor (Task 5 Step 6).

## Review Focus

1. **A slow or failed mall download** - the screen holds black with "Loading the mall..." and, on failure, comes back to the street outside the entrance with the mall withdrawn (walking into the door again does nothing). Pinned by e2e `a failed mall download returns to the street and withdraws the mall` (Task 5).
2. **Pressing a floor button mid-glide, or leaving mid-glide** - the glide retargets from where it is with no jump; Escape mid-glide still leaves for the street. Pinned by `pressing another floor mid-glide goes there from where the glide is` (Task 3) and machine `walkExit in the mall crosses to the street` (Task 4).
3. **Walking straight back in after leaving** - arriving outside facing out must not re-trigger the door; turning round and walking in must. Pinned by `leaving the mall lands outside the door, and walking back in reaches it` (Task 4).
4. **Escape, zoom-out or Damper during a crossing** - ignored, except a failed download retargeting the crossing to the street. Pinned by machine `during a crossing only crossed and a retarget apply` (Task 4).
5. **The floor panel on a phone** - its buttons must be tappable, not under the thumbstick or the back button. Pinned by e2e `the floor panel glides between floors and marks the current one` running on the mobile project too (Task 7; a covered button fails Playwright's click).

### Task 3: Mall simulation

**Files:**
- Create: `src/mall/simulation.ts`
- Test: `tests/unit/mallSimulation.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 2's `buildFloor`, `floorColliders`, `MALL_ENTRY`, `FLOOR_Y`, `CHARACTER`; `Walker`, `WalkInput`, `Phase`, `EYE_HEIGHT`, `eyePose`; `Crowd`, `buildGraph`, `Agent` (`src/street/crowd.ts`); `easeInOutCubic`, `lerpPose` (`src/tour/path.ts`).
- Produces:
  ```ts
  export const GLIDE_S = 1;
  export class MallSimulation {
    constructor(tenants: MallTenants, base: number);  // base: world y of the 1F floor
    readonly plans: Record<FloorId, FloorPlan>;
    floor(): FloorId;
    people(floor: FloorId): readonly Agent[];          // walkers and standers, the same objects every step
    seated(floor: FloorId): readonly Spot[];           // seats and queue: they never move
    visitor(): { x: number; z: number; yaw: number; pitch: number };
    eye(): Pose;                                       // mid-glide: the glide's pose
    landing(): Pose;                                   // eye pose at MALL_ENTRY on 1F
    reset(): void;                                     // to MALL_ENTRY on 1F, no glide
    place(x: number, z: number, yaw?: number, pitch?: number): void;  // on the current floor
    blocked(x: number, z: number): boolean;            // on the current floor
    goTo(floor: FloorId): void;                        // glide to that floor's lobby over GLIDE_S
    gliding(): boolean;
    step(dt: number, input?: WalkInput): void;         // dt capped at 0.1, like the street
    tick(phase: Phase, dt: number, input?: WalkInput): void;  // same meaning as StreetSimulation.tick
  }
  ```
  One `Walker` per floor (each with that floor's colliders, `ground = () => base + FLOOR_Y[f]`, home = the floor's lobby; 1F's home is `MALL_ENTRY`). Every floor's crowd steps every frame; only the current floor's crowd sees the visitor (others get a far-away visitor point). The visitor slides round the current floor's crowd, seated people and queue. During a glide input is ignored; the glide eases (`easeInOutCubic`) from the pose at the moment of `goTo` to the target lobby's eye pose; a second `goTo` restarts the glide from the current glide pose.

### Task 4: Crossing between places

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, in `src/tour/machine.ts`:
  ```ts
  export type PlaceId = 'street' | 'mall';
  export type Mode = 'tour' | 'explore' | 'returning' | 'descending' | 'walking' | 'crossing' | 'ascending';
  // TourState gains: crossingTo: PlaceId | 'damper' | null   (initial null)
  // TourEvent gains: { type: 'cross'; to: PlaceId | 'damper' } | { type: 'crossed' }
  ```
  Rules: `walking` + `cross{to}` with `to !== place` → `crossing`, `crossingTo = to`. `crossing` + `cross{to}` → retarget `crossingTo`. `crossing` + `crossed` → to a place: `walking`, `place = crossingTo`; to `'damper'`: `tour`, `place 'street'`, `progress = stopProgress(2)`, `segment = segmentAt(it)`, `activeHotspot null`. Either way `crossingTo = null`. `walkExit` while `walking` in the mall → `crossing` to `'street'`; on the street unchanged (ascend); during `crossing` ignored. `cross` in any other mode ignored. `crossing` is a walk mode: scroll, explore and hotspot events are ignored in it, like the other walk modes.
